Extend your memory with Memora

Memora smart glasses seamlessly capture and organize your daily experiences, providing instant access to audio recordings, photos, and key information. Never miss a moment or forget an important detail again.

Key Features

Memora glasses offer a range of features designed to enhance your memory and productivity.

Smart Audio Recording

Continuous audio recording with automatic quality adjustment when speech is detected.

Document Detection

Advanced OpenCV-powered detection of rectangular documents, papers, and screens with auto-capture.

AI Transcription

Whisper-powered automatic transcription of audio recordings with multiple format support.

Smart Document Processing

CamScanner-like document enhancement with corner detection and perspective correction.

Advanced Search

Search through transcripts, documents, and metadata with powerful filtering and sorting.

Appointment Extraction

AI-powered extraction of appointments, dates, and reminders from transcribed conversations.

Web Interface

Modern web-based interface for managing all your captured content with real-time updates.

How it Works

Memora glasses are designed for simplicity and ease of use.

1. Smart Capture

Continuous audio recording with voice detection and automatic document capture using OpenCV.

2. AI Processing

Whisper AI transcribes audio while CamScanner-like processing enhances document quality.

3. Smart Extraction

AI extracts appointments, dates, and key information from transcribed conversations automatically.

4. Web Dashboard

Access everything through the modern web interface with real-time updates and advanced search.
